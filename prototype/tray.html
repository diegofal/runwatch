Runwatch — tray

The 9 runners on this PC as three tray designs. Real setup copied here: 5 runners on the busy repo with ~80 jobs waiting, 1 on hooks, 3 on billing sitting idle. Hover the rows. Stop and re-run work in the simulation and never open a pop-up.

A Native menu

Right-click opens a standard Windows menu. Each runner has a submenu (terminal, stop, re-run). The lightest to build and the most familiar, but it can't show progress or a live terminal: those open in the dashboard.

B Flyout recommended

Left-click opens a small panel. Three numbers, one row per repo, and the running jobs with step and time. Click a job to see its terminal right there. Stop asks for confirmation inside the row. Right-click still opens menu A.

C Icon + notifications

No panel at all. The icon shows the state, hovering shows one line, and clicking opens the dashboard. You only hear from it when something happens: a job failed, or a job has waited more than 45 min. The notifications have Re-run and Log buttons.

What I'd build

B as the left click, A as the right click, and C's notifications for failures and long waits. The icon shows the queue: blue while jobs run, amber with the waiting count once something has waited more than 15 min, and a red dot when a job failed in the last hour. Everything that needs space (job history, queue table, full terminal, runner disk use) goes in the dashboard. The tray app serves it on localhost, so the same page also opens in a browser.
